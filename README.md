## Two-Shift Study Library (Phase 1: 21 Seats, Phase 2: +10–15 Seats)

### 1) Executive Summary
We will establish a **subscription-based Study Library** in our hometown providing a quiet, disciplined, comfortable study environment. The library will operate in **two shifts (8am–2pm and 2pm–8pm)** with **21 seats** initially and a planned expansion to **31–36 seats** once demand is proven.

- **Pricing:** ₹756 per seat per month for whole day shift  
- **Max memberships/month (Phase 1):** 21 seats × 2 shifts = **42**  
- **Max revenue/month (Phase 1):** **₹15,876**
- **Premises:** **Owned** (no rent)  
- **Summer AC:** Runs **8am–8pm** (electricity assumed ₹6,000/month)  
- **Funding Ask:** **₹4.5 lakh**

---

### 2) Problem & Opportunity
Many students and exam aspirants lack:
- a quiet, distraction-free study place at home,  
- stable electricity and comfortable seating,  
- disciplined environment and long study hours.  

A paid study library solves this with a low-ticket monthly plan and predictable recurring revenue.

---

### 3) Service Offering
**Core facilities**
- Dedicated desk/seat, good lighting, charging points  
- Clean washroom, drinking water  
- **Inverter backup**  
- **Wi‑Fi**  
- **CCTV surveillance**  
- **AC in summer** for comfort and retention  

**Operating schedule**
- Shift 1: 8:00 AM – 2:00 PM  
- Shift 2: 2:00 PM – 8:00 PM  
- 10-minute transition buffer at shift change for reset/cleanliness.

---

### 4) Pricing & Revenue Model
**Base membership price:** ₹500 / seat / month / shift  

**Capacity & revenue**
- Phase 1 seats: **21**  
- Max memberships/month: **42**  
- Max revenue/month: 42 × 500 = **₹21,000**  
- Max revenue/year: **₹2,52,000**  

(After market acceptance, optional upside: seasonal AC fee or premium AC seats—can be introduced without changing the base plan.)

---

### 5) Startup Investment (Phase 1 Capex)
Confirmed setup cost from your expense list:  
**Total Phase 1 Setup Cost: ₹3.25 lakh**

Includes:
- Submersible / water arrangement  
- Washrooms

**Use of funds:**

---

### 8) Financial Projections (Phase 1: 21 seats)
**Monthly profit at different occupancy (Max 42 memberships):**

| Occupancy | Memberships | Revenue | Opex | Net Profit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 50% | 21 | ₹10,500 | ₹8,500 | ₹2,000 |
| 70% | 30 | ₹15,000 | ₹8,500 | ₹6,500 |
| 80% | 34 | ₹17,000 | ₹8,500 | ₹8,500 |
| 90% | 38 | ₹19,000 | ₹8,500 | ₹10,500 |
| 100% | 42 | ₹21,000 | ₹8,500 | **₹12,500** |

**Break-even:**
- ₹8,500 ÷ ₹500 = **17 memberships/month** (~40% occupancy)

**Payback (on setup cost ₹3.25 lakh):**
- At 100% occupancy: ~**26 months**  
- At 80% occupancy: ~**38 months**  
- At 70% occupancy: ~**52 months**

---

### 9) Expansion Plan (Phase 2: add 10–15 seats)
We have additional back area available for construction to add **10–15 seats** once demand is stable.

**Expansion trigger:**
- **90–100% occupancy for 2–3 months** + waiting list/inquiries.

**Capacity and revenue after expansion (same pricing):**
- **31 seats:** 31×2 = 62 memberships → **₹31,000/month**  
- **36 seats:** 36×2 = 72 memberships → **₹36,000/month**

**Expected additional monthly costs (estimate):** +₹2,000 to +₹5,000  
(mainly electricity and upkeep)

**Incremental profit potential:**
- +10 seats: approx. **₹5,000–₹8,000/month** extra profit  
- +15 seats: approx. **₹9,000–₹12,000/month** extra profit

---

### 10) Marketing & Sales Strategy
**Customer acquisition**
- Tie-ups with local coaching centers (referral arrangement)  
- Posters near colleges, stationery shops, coaching hubs  
- WhatsApp groups + local social media page  
- Trial offers to fill seats fast (“2-day free trial” / “first week discounted”)

**Retention**
- Strict silent discipline + fixed seating  
- Clean washrooms and consistent cleanliness  
- Reliable power backup and Wi‑Fi  
- CCTV for safety and rule enforcement

---

### 11) Operations & Controls
- Membership register + seat allotment cards/receipts  
- Clear rules: silence, no phone calls, food restrictions, timings  
- Prevent overcrowding: only registered members in allotted shift  
- Maintenance schedule: weekly deep cleaning; monthly minor repairs check
